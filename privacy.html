Privacy policy

This is a concept demo site built by Tiyan Digital as an outreach preview. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What this demo collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party script on any page. The question form on the Order page is a demo — it validates in your browser and never sends your details anywhere.

What the real site would collect

When this concept becomes the bakery's real website, it would collect only what an order needs: your name, contact details, and order. It would say so on the form itself, and ask your permission before replying.

Your rights

Because this demo stores nothing about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about the demo itself, contact Tiyan Digital.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date. Last updated: November 2026.
