Good to know

A lake-town
pickup ritual.

Order by the cutoff on the bake-week board, and your bread is baked to order — not pulled from a shelf. Bring a bag; the loaves travel best in paper.

Coming from out of town? Wolfeboro sits on Lake Winnipesaukee in New Hampshire's Lakes Region — the pickup pairs well with a lakeside evening.

No invented address. We won't publish a pickup point we don't have. The bakery confirms the exact spot with every order.
